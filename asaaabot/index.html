the crew

how a brick gets laid

Systems instead of duct tape. Same path every time.

  1. An ideaThe Captain radios it in, however cryptic. The idea was step 1.
  2. DecodedTower writes it in plain words, then slots it: a tweak, a dream, a task or a question.
  3. PlacedBig stuff lands on the Board with a finish line and a path. Small stuff goes to a bot.
  4. BuiltA bot lays the brick and checks its own work before calling it done.
  5. CheckedA human checks the AI's work. Anything that needs a human click gets a purple buddy.
  6. LoggedEvery brick leaves a trail. The engine writes it down in plain words.

the rules on the wall

For every asaaabot and every human. Same rules, same wall.

Check your own work.It's everyone's responsibility. Bots included.
Humans check the AI's work.Bots do the labor. People have the final look.
Systems, not duct tape.Magnets to move stuff around, superglue to glue it on. Why? Because it works.
The purple buddy rule.Money, posting, accounts, codes and live settings are a human's click. The bot sets it up and points.
Never delete anything.Retire it, label it, keep it. Abandoned scaffolding might snap into something later.
Humans fingerpaint.No AI-generated art anywhere. Bots build the frame, people make the picture.
Leave a trail.What you did goes somewhere a human can read it. If you can't finish, leave a handoff.
Think big, work small.Asked to think, think big. Asked to work, work small. Stuck? Step back and find the finish line.

the purple buddy

How a bot asks a human for a hand.

Some steps should always be a person's: anything that spends money, posts in public, or touches an account or a password. An asaaabot never takes those steps for you.

Instead it does all the setup, opens the right page, fills in what it can, and pins a purple buddy next to the one button that's yours. Then it waits.

One click from a human, and the structure keeps building.

  2. Click the glowing Add button
  3. Tell the bot it's done
The bot did the rest. You make the call.
