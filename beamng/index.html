Drive asaaab in BeamNG

The Angelish Crest server runs on BeamMP, the free multiplayer mod for BeamNG.drive. Not exactly Angeles Crest. On purpose.

  1. You need BeamNG.drive on a PC. It's not on PS5 or Xbox yet.
  2. Get BeamMP from beammp.com and open its launcher. Help lives at docs.beammp.com.
  3. Find "Angelish Crest" in the BeamMP server list and join. Direct-connect details are pinned in the hangar.
  4. House rules: one car per player.

Stuck, or got an idea for the server? Suggestions go in the asaaab hangar.

No PC? No problem.

Go carspotting in your browser. Any phone, any laptop.
