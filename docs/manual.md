# Co-Owner's Manual
For co-owners.

## Body structure
Two A-pillars, two B-pillars, two C-pillars, two D-pillars. It's asaaab.

A: Take care of the people putting in the work. | Oxygen mask first. Self care.
B: Everyone's paid the same. | Everyone takes home the same split of profits per hour invested.
C: If you want more growth from asaaab and it's not more growth from the current thing, invest in your people. People are what made Saab great, people will make asaaab thrive. | It sustains the people it employs well enough they can take on their own whimsy endeavours, using the resources they are a co-owner in, whether it be content or shark tanking their idea to the board.
D: A break even model. Capped growth potential and contentment. Good enough. | Equal work for equal pay.
ROOF: There is always a why.
