a tool, not a vibe

im sick...of this place.

anonymous symptom check-ins for your whole break room. see how many coworkers feel it too. nobody has to be the first to call out alone.

the idea, in one breath

you feel kinda meh. you could call out. but is it just you? it never is. this tells you three of your coworkers feel it too, and lets you be the first to pull the bell.

how it works

  1. 1

    someone puts the poster up

    in the break room, by the time clock, wherever the boss doesn't look. it has your workplace code on it.

  2. 2

    you join with the code

    no name, no email, no login the company can see. the code proves you're a coworker. that's the whole point of it being on paper in the break room and not on the internet.

  3. 3

    feeling meh? tap it.

    you see how many coworkers checked in feeling symptoms today. a number, never a name. the company never sees any of it.

  4. 4

    pull the im sick bell

    it drafts the message to your boss. you copy it, you send it, you stay home. your coworkers see the bell got pulled and decide for themselves.

why a code on a poster

anything on the open internet, your boss can join. a code taped up in the break room, only the people in the room can read. share it with your people. that's your union, whether or not it has a name yet.

what it isn't

  • not a doctor. it never tells you what you have or what to take. feeling bad is enough to count.
  • not the company's. no HR login, no manager dashboard, no exports. the company doesn't get a copy because the company isn't in the room.
  • not a snitch list. counts, never names. you can't see who, and neither can we.
  • not a strike. it's a bell. what people do when it rings is theirs.
