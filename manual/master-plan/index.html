The guardrail ring

Every process above runs inside these. They come from the Computer Use Policy and the standing rules.

  • Build it once, good enough. Improve when needed.
  • Low resource use. A simpler method wins.
  • No AI-generated photos or art. Humans fingerpaint.
  • Computers never post, publish or pay without a human yes.
  • No one's home, neighborhood or personal data in public.
  • Words stay verbatim. Distill, don't rewrite.
  • Don't measure success by output or growth.
  • Nothing gets deleted. Old versions get archived.
