Privacy policy

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no cookies, nothing sold. This site keeps almost nothing about you, and what it does keep can't point back to you.

1Visit counts

asaaab.com counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It records totals like which pages were viewed, which site sent the visit, browser and screen size, rough country, and a few button taps (like Start carspotting, Fast travel or Share). It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address, and keeps only combined numbers, not a record of you.

2What's saved in your browser

One random number, saved in your browser's local storage, so the cars parked along your route stay the same each time you come back. It never leaves your device. Tap New drive, new cars at the end of the drive, or clear this site's data, to get a new one.

3Your passport

The passport picture is drawn right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. It only goes somewhere if you choose to save or share it.

4Other services

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them, like it would on most sites.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic request info (such as IP address) for security and reliability.
  • Links out to YouTube, TikTok, Discord, Bluesky and others take you to their sites, which have their own privacy policies.

5Email

If you email asaaab93x@gmail.com, your address and message are used to reply to you. That's it. No mailing lists unless you ask to be on one.

6Kids

This site isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. (It barely collects anything from anyone.)

7Changes & questions

If this policy changes, the date below changes with it. Questions: asaaab93x@gmail.com.
